I'm Jackelyn and I love learning about the world at large and how we are all connected by those pesky invisible threads. Some of my interests include film, creative writing, and fiber arts.

I matriculated at Wayne State University and pursued a B.S. in Business Administration with majors in Global Supply Chain Management and Finance. After graduating in 2023 I attended the Apple Developer Academy, where I deepened my understanding of app development and discovered a love for narrative and product storytelling. 

As the design landscape is constantly evolving, my philosophy has remained the same: keeping people at the center of the product. While metrics play a huge role in product decisions, it's equally important to design for how people's needs shift and evolve.
